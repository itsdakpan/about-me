Me in a nutshell
I'm a son, a brother, a friend, an anime lover, a gamer, a coffee enthusiast, and an engineer who became a software developer.

Back Story.

Since I was a kid, I knew what I wanted to be: an engineer. Building things just felt natural to me, so I spent years in school becoming one.

Then I got there, and slowly realised it wasn't quite my calling. Engineering is slow. There are a lot of processes to follow, and not much room to be creative.

Then I found code, and it unlocked a kind of creativity and flow I'd never had before. You can make something real in a short time, especially now with AI, and once it's live, loads of people can use what you built. My team once built an app in two and a half hours and won a hackathon with it.

That's what I love about it. It's fulfilling, it's exciting, and I'm still the kid who just wants to build things. The rest of the story's better over a coffee, so come and say hi.
